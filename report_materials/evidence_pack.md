## 1. Architecture: Bi-Encoder vs Cross-Encoder (facts from this project)

- **Bi-Encoder:** `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`.
  - Query and chunk are encoded **separately** into 384-dimensional vectors, then L2-normalised.
  - Relevance = inner product = cosine similarity (FAISS `IndexFlatIP`).
- **Cross-Encoder:** `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`.
  - Each (query, chunk) pair is fed **together** as one input, so self-attention spans both texts.
  - Output: one unbounded relevance logit per pair.
- **Measured on this machine:**
  - Embedding all 85 chunks once (offline, cached): 2162 ms (≈ 25.4 ms per chunk).
  - Embedding one query: 10.0 ms (median).
  - FAISS search: 0.069 ms.
  - Cross-Encoder on 10 pairs: 253 ms (median).
  - Nothing can be cached for the Cross-Encoder, because each pair depends on the query.
- **Score scales differ:**
  - Cosine similarities in our top-10 lists lie between 0.226 and 0.811.
  - Cross-Encoder logits lie between -11.28 and 9.46.
  - Only rank orders are comparable.
